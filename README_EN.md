🚀 **Welcome to the future of operating systems!** 🚀

**ZetaTron-OS-64** is the most ambitious and innovative 64-bit operating system project ever developed by **SonnerStudio**. The days of frustrating compatibility issues, virtual machines, and switching between different platforms are about to end.

Our system is led by our powerful mascot: The **hornet ZetaTron**. It perfectly represents what this OS stands for – unmatched speed, pure precision, and unstoppable power! 🐝⚡

### 🌍 Mission and Ultimate Goal
Imagine having one single operating system that can do it *all*. Our ultimate goal with ZetaTron-OS-64 is to create a boundless, truly universal platform.

What does that mean? Our revolutionary 64-bit system is designed from the ground up to seamlessly run software from all major ecosystems **natively within one single environment**. This includes:
- 🔸 **Windows**
- 🔸 **macOS**
- 🔸 **Android**
- 🔸 **Linux distributions**
- 🔸 **SonnerStudio Software**

No more compromises. One system for all your applications.
